Conclusion: JMPR lands on a 32-bit register address with no 16-bit range limit.
The probe is discriminating because a failed/ignored JMPR executes instr 4-5,
exits 60 with W5 = 0, and never reaches byte 0x8000; the observed run instead
shows W5 = 777, W8 = W9 = 10 and PC = 32780, i.e. the success path at 0x8000
ran. W0 = 0x8000 also proves the address was built in-register (MV+SHL), since
no single MV holds it positive.
